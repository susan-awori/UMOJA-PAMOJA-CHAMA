

# **CONSTITUTION AND BYLAWS OF UMOJA PAMOJA CHAMA**

**Date of Adoption:** January 10, 2026

**Registration Number:** OSH/2026/NBI-99482

---

## **SECTION 1: NAME AND OBJECTIVES**

1.1 The name of this group shall be **Umoja Pamoja Chama** (hereinafter referred to as "the Chama").

1.2 The primary objective of the Chama is to promote economic empowerment among its members through monthly savings, a revolving fund (Merry-Go-Round), and welfare support.

---

## **SECTION 2: MEMBERSHIP AND CONTRIBUTIONS**

2.1 The Chama shall consist of a maximum of 15 registered members.

2.2 Every member must contribute a monthly non-refundable savings amount of **KES 5,000** .

2.3 Every member must contribute **KES 2,000** monthly toward the Merry-Go-Round fund.

2.4 Every member must contribute **KES 500** monthly toward the Welfare Fund.

2.5 All monthly contributions must be sent via M-Pesa to the designated Treasurer's number by the **5th day of every month** at 6:00 PM.

---

## **SECTION 3: MERRY-GO-ROUND ROTATION (CHAMA POOL)**

3.1 The total Merry-Go-Round collection of **KES 30,000** (KES 2,000 \* 15 members) shall be paid out to one member every month on a rotational basis.

3.2 The rotation order is determined by a random ballot draw at the beginning of the year and cannot be altered unless under emergency circumstances approved by the executive committee.

3.3 A member cannot receive the Merry-Go-Round payout if they have outstanding arrears or unpaid fines.

---

## **SECTION 4: WELFARE FUND AND EMERGENCY PAYOUTS**

4.1 The Welfare Fund is strictly reserved for bereavement, hospitalization, or weddings of an official member, their legal spouse, or biological children.

4.2 In the event of the hospitalization of a member lasting more than three (3) consecutive nights, the Chama shall issue a non-refundable welfare payout of **KES 10,000**.

4.3 In the event of bereavement (loss of immediate family as defined in 4.1), the Chama shall issue a payout of **KES 20,000** to assist with funeral expenses.

4.4 Any welfare claim must be accompanied by supporting documentation (e.g., a hospital discharge summary or a burial permit) submitted to the Secretary within 14 days of the event.

---

## **SECTION 5: FINES, PENALTIES, AND DISCIPLINE**

5.1 **Late Contribution Fine:** Any member who fails to send their monthly contribution by the 5th day deadline shall incur an automatic late fine of **KES 500**.

5.2 **Meeting Attendance:** Attendance at the bi-monthly physical/virtual meetings is mandatory.

* Failure to attend a meeting without an apology submitted 24 hours in advance attracts a fine of **KES 200**.  
* Arriving more than 30 minutes late to a meeting attracts a fine of **KES 100**.  
* 5.3 **Defaulting on Payments:** If a member fails to make their monthly contributions for three (3) consecutive months, they will be suspended from the Chama. Their accumulated savings will be frozen, and they will forfeit their right to any Merry-Go-Round payouts until all arrears and accumulated fines are paid in full.

---

## **SECTION 6: DISPUTE RESOLUTION AND ARBITRATION**

6.1 In the event of a disagreement regarding financial records, payout order, or the calculation of fines, the matter must first be table-talked calmly with the Treasurer and Secretary.

6.2 If a resolution cannot be reached internally, the Chama shall refer to the official **AI Arbitration Agent** to query the records and provide an objective ruling based strictly on these bylaws and verifiable M-Pesa statement evidence. The AI's citation of these bylaws shall be deemed final for immediate dispute mitigation.
